Our Compliance Process

01
Job Assignment & Scheduling
After a customer agrees to the upgrade, job details are assigned to installers or subcontractors
02
Live Audit Monitoring
We coordinate with installers to ensure they follow VEU program rules. we track live audits and ensure installers respond if an auditor visits the site
03
Appointment Verification
Before work starts, all mandatory appointment questions in the Dataforce or Alisty portal are completed.
04
Collection of Document
We review each photo to ensure it meets VEU and audit standards. Installers must capture all required photos
05
Preparing COES & VBA
For electrical or plumbing-based installations, we assist installers in preparing COES and VBA compliance certificates, ensuring no details are missing.
06
100-Point ID Verification
We collect valid identity documents to match VEU requirements. Driver’s license, passport, or utility bill.ASIC details for businesses.
07
Job Submission to VEU Portal
Once all documents are verified, we upload the job into the VEU portal. This includes data entry, product details, customer info and energy savings calculations.
08
Handling All RFIs
If the VEU portal raises an RFI, we quickly resolve it by correcting documents, adding missing photos, or clarifying installer details to get approval without delay.
09
Submitting 20% of Field Audit
As per VEU guidelines, 20% of completed jobs must be submitted for independent field audits. We select, prepare,submit audit files, ensuring full compliance.
10
Archiving & Record Keeping
All job files photos, IDs, certificates, RFIs, emails, and invoices are securely stored for future audits, company records, and regulatory checks.
