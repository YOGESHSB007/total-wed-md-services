Our Compliance Process

01
Initial Assessment
We conduct a thorough review of your solar or battery installation to ensure all components meet eligibility criteria for available rebates and incentives. Our team verifies system specifications, installation quality, and compliance with current regulations.
02
Documentation Collection
Our experts gather and organize all necessary documentation including product certifications, installation photos, electrical schematics, and customer details. We ensure every document meets the strict requirements of rebate administrators.
03
Application Preparation
We meticulously prepare your rebate application, cross-referencing all data points and ensuring accuracy. Our team stays updated on the latest scheme requirements and submission protocols to maximize approval chances.
04
Submission & Tracking
Once approved internally, we submit your application through the appropriate channels and provide you with real-time tracking updates. We monitor the application status and respond promptly to any queries from administrators.
05
Approval & Payment
We manage the final approval process and coordinate payment processing. Our team ensures you receive your full rebate entitlement and provides comprehensive documentation for your records and future reference.
