FAQ's

?

What is digital marketing and why do I need it?

Digital marketing encompasses all online marketing efforts. It's essential because your customers are online—searching, shopping, and engaging on digital platforms every day.

?

How long does it take to see results from digital marketing?

Results vary by strategy. PPC campaigns can show immediate results, while SEO typically takes 3-6 months. We focus on sustainable, long-term growth alongside quick wins.

?

What's the difference between SEO and PPC?

SEO improves organic search rankings over time without paying per click. PPC delivers immediate visibility but requires payment for each click. Both are valuable and complementary.

?

How do you measure digital marketing success?

We track KPIs aligned with your goals: traffic, conversions, ROI, engagement rates, and more. You'll receive regular reports with clear metrics and actionable insights.

?

What's your pricing structure for digital marketing services?

We offer flexible packages tailored to your needs and budget. Pricing depends on services, campaign scope, and goals. Contact us for a custom quote based on your requirements.

?

Do I need to be on all social media platforms?

Not necessarily. We help identify which platforms your target audience uses most and focus efforts there. Quality presence on the right platforms beats mediocre presence everywhere.
