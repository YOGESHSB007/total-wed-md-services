Understanding Solar & Battery Rebates

Navigate the complex landscape of renewable energy incentives with confidence

STCs (Small-scale Technology Certificates)

Federal government incentive that reduces upfront costs for solar installations. We handle the complex calculation and assignment process to maximize your benefit.

State Battery Rebates

Various state governments offer substantial rebates for battery storage systems. Our team navigates different state requirements to secure maximum returns.

Feed-in Tariffs

Export excess solar energy back to the grid for credit. We ensure your installation qualifies and help you choose the best tariff for maximum savings.
