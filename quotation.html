FAQ's

?

Which platform is better for my business - Zoho or Xero?

It depends on your needs. Zoho is ideal for businesses seeking comprehensive CRM integration and customization. Xero excels for accounting-focused operations. We'll help you choose the perfect fit during our consultation.

?

How long does the implementation take?

Most implementations are completed within 48-72 hours. This includes setup, data migration, template creation, and team training. Complex integrations may take up to a week.

?

Can I migrate my existing quotes?

Yes! We provide complete data migration services. All your existing quotes, products, customers, and pricing information will be securely transferred to your new platform.

?

Do you provide training for my team?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive training sessions covering all features, from basic quote creation to advanced automation. Training materials and ongoing support are included.

?

Can quotes be converted to invoices automatically?

Yes! Both Zoho and Xero allow instant conversion of accepted quotes to invoices. This eliminates duplicate data entry and speeds up your billing process significantly.

?

What kind of support do you offer after implementation?

We provide 24/7 support via email and chat, plus dedicated account managers for premium clients. Monthly check-ins and system optimization are included in all packages.
