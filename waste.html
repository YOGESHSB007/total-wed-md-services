Our Compliance Process

01
Pre-Audit Call
We begin with a data verification call to validate product details and customer information, preventing future claim rejections. Our specialists cross-check every detail of product specs, installation criteria, and customer data before work begins.
02
Job Assignment & Installer Coordination
We assign jobs, guide installers, and make sure communication between all parties stays smooth and documented. Assigning jobs to coordinating with installers, we keep every stakeholder aligned and accountable.
03
Live Compliance Checks & Audits
Our auditors conduct live on-site or virtual checks and capture real-time evidence (photos, serials, signatures, forms) that meet current compliance laws and scheme guidelines. This is where we truly set ourselves apart.
04
Documentation & Certification
We review all collected data, compile complete evidence, and submit jobs with zero margin for error. We know what auditors look for, and we prepare each file to exceed those expectations, reducing the chance of rejection to near zero.
05
Post-Installation Client Call
Our work doesn’t end when the tools are packed up. Once the installation is done, our team calls the customer to confirm satisfaction, gather feedback, and verify that the product is working as intended.
